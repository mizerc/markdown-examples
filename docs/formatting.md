# Formatting

## Bold

Result:

**Boost your development and feel free to use your imagination!**

Syntax:

```text
**Boost your development and feel free to use your imagination!**
```

## Italic

Result:

_Boost your development and feel free to use your imagination!_

Syntax:

```text
*Boost your development and feel free to use your imagination!*
```

## Bold and italic

Result:

**_Boost your development and feel free to use your imagination!_**

Syntax:

```text
***Boost your development and feel free to use your imagination!***
```

## Strikethrough

Result:

~~Boost your development and feel free to use your imagination!~~

Syntax:

```text
~~Boost your development and feel free to use your imagination!~~
```

## Inline code

Result:

`Boost your development and feel free to use your imagination!`

Syntax:

```text
`Boost your development and feel free to use your imagination!`
```

## Blockquote

Result:

> Boost your development and feel free to use your imagination!

Syntax:

```text
> Boost your development and feel free to use your imagination!
```

## Subscript

Result:

Boost your development<sub>and feel free to use your imagination!</sub>

Syntax:

```text
Boost your development<sub>and feel free to use your imagination!</sub>
```

## Superscript

Result:

Boost your development<sup>and feel free to use your imagination!</sup>

Syntax:

```text
Boost your development<sup>and feel free to use your imagination!</sup>
```
